# AI Trends: approved outline (Zulu AI Working Group · December 2026)

Copy below is approved. Use it word for word on slides. Motion notes describe the one idea each slide animates.

## Through-line and core message
The talk moves in three beats. They're said once at the top, echoed at the turn in section 2, and closed in section 6:
1. **It talked.** AI as a chat partner. We learned to prompt.
2. **It made.** Generated images, video and copy. We learned to make with it.
3. **It does.** Agents open our tools and finish the job. We're learning to hand off the work.

Under all three sits the belief the talk rests on: creativity that means something still starts with a person. AI takes on more of the doing every year. The idea, the taste and the reason to make something stay human.
