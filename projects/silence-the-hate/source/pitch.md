# The pitch: "Z.A.K. x PFLAG V2" (text of the deck)

Source: `ZAK-x-PFLAG-V2.pptx` (14 slides, no speaker notes). Frames from the key slides are in `frames/`.

1. **pflag x zulu**
2. **Why we're here.** As a leading voice in 2SLGBTQIA+ allyship, pflag canada is the ideal partner to turn harmful lyrics into opportunities for education, awareness and positive change.
3. Music has always brought together the 2SLGBTQIA+ community and the people who love them.
4. But music can divide as much as it can unite.
5. Homophobia and transphobia are still everywhere in people's playlists.
6. And as long as these songs keep playing, platforms and artists keep profiting from hate.
7. Launching on May 17, International Day Against Homophobia, Biphobia and Transphobia, we'll push the industry towards a more inclusive future.
8. **Introducing: Silence The Hate** (title card with the pflag canada lock-up). `frames/01-title.jpg`
9. **What it is.** The tool that skips homophobia and transphobia in music, turning every skip into an opportunity for education. `frames/05-what-it-is.jpg`
10. **How it works.**
    - **Install.** People are directed to a microsite where they can learn about the initiative and download the plugin.
    - **Listen.** Once installed, the plugin automatically skips problematic songs without you noticing, turning your music algorithm into a safe space. `frames/02-plugin-on.jpg` ("Our plugin is now active and will automatically skip songs with homophobic and transphobic lyrics." + ON toggle)
    - **Educate.** Users can explore flagged songs and learn why certain lyrics are hurtful, and be redirected to pflag resources where they can learn more.
11. **Flagging lyrics.** We'll flag individual sentences or words in the song and include information where they can learn more about the importance of choosing the right words. `frames/03-flagged-lyrics.jpg` (flagged line repeated, "Words matter" note, a "Deep dive" resource card linking to a pflag article, Back / Next pager)
12. **Potential impact.**
    - **Generating donations.** We could leverage pflag canada's corporate partnerships to match the value of every skipped stream with a donation, redirecting it towards positive change.
    - **Igniting conversations.** We expect this initiative to generate significant earned media, attracting attention towards pflag canada starting a conversation around who benefits.
    - **Creating change.** Everyone who downloads and uses the plug-in unlocks an exclusive playlist celebrating inclusive music and artists.
13. "pflag canada is turning harmful lyrics into opportunities for education."
14. Thank you.

Footer on the closing slides: property of Zulu Alpha Kilo; ZAK owns the thinking unless the partnership goes ahead.

## What the mock-ups imply for the build

- App header shows service icons (a cloud icon, a drive-like icon) next to the plugin icon: the pitch assumes it works with more than one music service.
- The education view is a pager through flagged lines with a short "words matter" explanation and a linked pflag resource card ("Deep dive", read time).
- "Without you noticing" sets a bar: the skip has to happen within the first second or so of a flagged track.
